Antigravity quota and limits

Updated 4 November 2026. Limits change; the sources below have the current numbers.

Antigravity gives each plan a baseline quota per model. On Google AI Pro and Ultra it refreshes every five hours, with a weekly rate limit on top. Other plans get a quota that refreshes weekly.

How the quota works

  • Google AI Ultra: "The highest, most generous quota, refreshed every five hours", plus the highest weekly rate limits.
  • Google AI Pro: "A high, generous quota, refreshed every five hours until the weekly limit is reached."
  • Other plans: "A meaningful quota, refreshed weekly."
  • It counts work, not prompts. Google says "more complex tasks consume more quota".
  • Per model. Limits differ by model and plan. On Pro and Ultra, AI credits can take over once a model's baseline quota runs out, depending on your AI Credit Overages setting.

How to check it

In Antigravity, the Settings page shows baseline quota usage across models. It's accurate, but it means leaving your work to go and look.

Keep it on screen instead

Agent Notch reads your Antigravity quota with the Google sign-in already on your computer and shows the model that is closest to running out, so the ring warns you before any model stops. If the sign-in expires, the ring says login instead of showing an old number.
